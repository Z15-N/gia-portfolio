About me

From Ho Chi Minh City to Ultimo, by way of a lot of maths.

I grew up in Ho Chi Minh City and moved to Sydney in 2026 to study Artificial Intelligence at the University of Technology Sydney. First year is mostly the maths underneath AI: statistics, linear algebra and algorithmic problem-solving. That suits me, because maths is the thing I've always been best at.

Outside class I tutor two students every day, one in Year 1 and one in Year 7. Before Sydney I worked front of house in a busy restaurant and on the register at a convenience store, which taught me to stay calm when twelve things happen at once.

On campus I'm on the marketing team at AIESEC in UTS, where our Instagram push sold out a mentoring event, and I was selected for the UTS Consulting Society's Aspiring Consultant Program.
